🧙 The Lost Amulet of Eldergrove

A great evil has stolen the Amulet of Eldergrove and hidden it deep within the dungeon. Your quest: find the Amulet, then escape through the exit. The exit is locked — find the Key to open it. Beware the monsters, traps and swamps; a Sword and Health Potions will help you survive.

Controls

  • W A S D / Arrow keys — move
  • E / Enter — pick up items
  • 1–4 — use an inventory item
  • F — toggle fullscreen
